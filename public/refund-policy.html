Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 Scope: Campus Bus Passes, Transit Subscriptions & Transport Fees
Transit Fee Transparency: This policy governs the refund, cancellation, and fee adjustment guidelines for campus bus transportation fees, semester passes, and shuttle route subscriptions administered through the College Bus Route Optimizer platform.

1. Eligibility for Transportation Fee Refunds

Refunds or prorated adjustments may be requested under the following recognized conditions:

  • Formal Academic Withdrawal: Enrolled students who formally withdraw from college academic programs within the first 14 calendar days of a semester are eligible for a prorated refund of the remaining semester bus fee, minus a nominal administrative processing fee.
  • Permanent Route Discontinuation: If the college administration permanently suspends a scheduled bus route or pickup point and cannot provide a reasonable alternate route within 1.5 km of your stop, a full prorated refund for the remainder of the term will be issued automatically.
  • Accidental Duplicate Payment: If a student or parent is billed twice for the same transit pass or semester subscription, the duplicate transaction will be refunded in full upon verification.

2. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will not be granted under the following scenarios:

  • Temporary transit disruptions caused by inclement weather, road construction, city detours, or public transit strikes.
  • Student non-attendance, voluntary missed rides, holidays, or personal schedule changes.
  • Suspension or termination of transportation privileges resulting from code-of-conduct violations, terminal intrusion attempts, or bus vandalism.
  • Requests submitted after the conclusion of the academic semester.

3. Refund Request Procedure

To request a bus fee adjustment or refund, please submit a written application to the Transport Accounts Desk:

  1. Send an email to saiprakash9813@gmail.com with the subject line: [Refund Request] - Student Name - ID Number.
  2. Attach proof of transit fee payment and official documentation supporting the cancellation (e.g., college withdrawal slip or relocation certificate).
  3. The Transport Committee reviews all requests within 5 business days and notifies the applicant in writing.

4. Disbursement Timelines

Approved refunds will be processed via electronic bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/UPI) to the student's designated bank account or original payment instrument within 7 to 10 working days from the date of formal approval.
