Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 Compliance: India's DPDP Act, 2023 & Global Privacy Standards
Cookie Transparency: We only use cookies and browser storage strictly required for authentication and security, plus optional anonymous visit counters to balance transit route server loads. We do not use any commercial advertising trackers.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and HTML5 Local Storage are small text files or key-value entries stored directly in your web browser. They allow our web application to recognize your session as you navigate across bus stops, view route graphs, and check live schedules without asking you to sign in on every single page.

2. Categories of Storage We Use

Storage Key Category Purpose Duration
token Strictly Necessary Stores your encrypted JSON Web Token (JWT) session to verify authorized transit access. 24 Hours / Logout
user Strictly Necessary Stores display name and role (Student / Staff / Admin) to render appropriate navigation links. Session / Logout
cookie_consent Strictly Necessary Remembers your affirmative privacy preference ('all' vs 'essential'). 1 Year
visitor_logs Optional Analytics Anonymized page load counters (with IP masked) to optimize route server bandwidth. 30 Days

3. Do You Need Cookie Consent?

Yes. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, personal data or usage indicators may only be processed on the basis of informed consent or specific legitimate uses.

While strictly necessary authentication tokens do not require consent because the application cannot function without them, we provide a proactive Cookie Consent Banner for all first-time visitors, enabling you to choose whether optional analytics and telemetry logging are enabled or disabled.
