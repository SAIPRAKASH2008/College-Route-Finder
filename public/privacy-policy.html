2. Data We Collect & Specific Processing Purposes

In accordance with the principle of data minimization (Section 4, DPDP Act), we only collect data that is strictly necessary for transit services and security:

  • Student & User Accounts: Full name, institutional or personal email address, salted password hash (bcrypt), and account privilege level (Student/User). Purpose: Authenticating transit access and saving personal bus routes.
  • Staff & Administrator Accounts: Name, college email, password hash, and Google Authenticator Two-Factor (2FA) TOTP secret key encrypted with AES-256-CBC. Purpose: Enforcing administrative clearance and securing route graph data.
  • Biometric Terminal Camera Snapshots: A webcam photo captured strictly upon unauthorized or repeated failed login attempts at administrative clearance terminals. Purpose: Legitimate security use under Section 4 and Section 7 of the DPDP Act for deterrence of unauthorized terminal tampering, dispatched immediately to the security administrator at saiprakash9813@gmail.com.
  • Technical Logs: Anonymized IP addresses (with the final octet masked, e.g., 192.168.1.0), browser user-agent header, and referrer URL. Purpose: System performance, debugging, and traffic analytics.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following lawful grounds established by the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • Consent (Section 6): Affirmative, free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent provided when registering an account, subscribing to transit updates, or enabling optional analytics cookies.
  • Legitimate Uses (Section 7): Protecting terminal infrastructure, investigating unauthorized intrusion breaches, enforcing rate limits, and ensuring institutional campus safety.

4. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal in India, you enjoy the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access Information (Section 11): You may request a summary of your personal data processed by us and the identities of any processors.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): You may request the correction of inaccurate data or the permanent deletion of your account and associated route logs.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): You have the right to register a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer and receive timely resolution.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): You have the right to designate an authorized nominee in accordance with DPDP rules in case of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for optional analytics or cancel your user account at any time without penalty.

5. Data Security & Storage Standards

We employ robust technical and organizational security controls to protect your data:

  • Encryption: TOTP secrets are encrypted at rest using AES-256-CBC with a secure 32-byte key. Passwords are irreversibly hashed using bcrypt.
  • Brute Force Lockout: Automated rate limiting locks out IP addresses and accounts after failed attempts to prevent unauthorized password guessing.
  • Access Control: Granular role-based authorization separates Student transit tools from Administrator graph editing endpoints.

6. Third-Party Services & Map Providers

Our application integrates with OpenStreetMap and Leaflet for geospatial coordinate visualization. Map tiles are fetched directly from OpenStreetMap servers subject to the OpenStreetMap Copyright & License. No student personal information or student travel history is shared with map providers.
